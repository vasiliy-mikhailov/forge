## Key findings

1. **MoE active-params dominates decode speed.** gpt-oss-20b (3.6B active) is the new fastest model in the fleet at **255 tok/s peak** — beats Qwen 2.5-7B (214) despite 3× the total params. gpt-oss-120b (5.1B active) hits 150 tok/s — between Llama 3.1-8B and Mistral-Nemo-12B. The total-param-count metric isn't useful here; only active-per-token matters for decode.
2. **Native MXFP4 prefill is exceptional.** gpt-oss-20b prefill = **107 K tok/s**, gpt-oss-120b = **76 K tok/s**. Both massively above same-active-class NVFP4 models. The Marlin MXFP4 backend in vLLM 0.20.0 is well-optimized.
3. **TRITON_ATTN is a 4× decode penalty.** Devstral-2 123B at 15 tok/s decode vs the 35-50 tok/s a similarly-sized model would get on FlashInfer. TRITON_ATTN was forced because FlashInfer hits a workspace buffer overflow on 70 GB+ models. Until we lower max_model_len enough to fit the FlashInfer workspace (or build a vLLM with bigger workspace allocation), 100B+ NVFP4 models pay this tax.
4. **Nemotron 3 Super 120B at 91 tok/s decode** is much faster than Nemotron-Super 49B v1.5 (35 tok/s). The Mamba-hybrid arch in v3 plus MoE routing produces real speed wins despite being 2.5× the total params.

## Failures and notes

- **gpt-oss-120b emits stop tokens early.** With `--ignore-eos`, the bench client expects 5120 output tokens per prompt but the model produces only ~40-50 tokens before stopping. We worked around this by reading TPOT (18.4 ms = 54 tok/s sustained) and decode peak (150 tok/s) directly from the bench output rather than relying on the throughput-per-prompt aggregate.
- **Devstral-2 123B was the slowest decode in the entire fleet** (15 tok/s) — entirely a backend artifact, not a model-quality signal. Same model on FlashInfer would likely be 35-45 tok/s.
- **Nemotron-3 Super 120B requires Mamba-cache config.** vLLM's default `--max-num-seqs=1024` exceeds available Mamba cache blocks; needed `--max-num-seqs=64` for graph capture.

## Methodology notes

- All cells share max_model_len=32768, kv_cache_dtype=fp8, gpu_memory_utilization=0.85-0.92, tensor_parallel_size=1 (solo Blackwell).
- Devstral-123B + Nemotron-120B used `--attention-backend TRITON_ATTN` to avoid FlashInfer workspace overflow at this size.
- gpt-oss models use MXFP4 (native), not NVFP4 — vLLM auto-selects the Marlin backend.
- Bench client: `vllm bench serve --backend openai-chat --base-url http://vllm-inference:8000 --tokenizer <hf_path> --trust-remote-code --ignore-eos`.
